Zesty Lemon Garlic Salmon

A restaurant-quality dish featuring perfectly seared salmon fillets bathed in a buttery lemon-garlic sauce, served with roasted asparagus and sweet cherry tomatoes.

⚡ The Process

1
🥩

PREP

Pat salmon dry. Season generously with salt and pepper. Mince garlic finely.

2
🔥

SEAR

Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add salmon, skin-side up, and cook for 4 minutes until golden.

3
🥄

BASTE

Flip salmon. Add butter, garlic, and lemon juice to the pan. Sauté 1 min. Constantly baste salmon with the sauce until cooked through.

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Prepare the Salmon

Remove salmon from refrigerator 15 minutes before cooking to bring to room temperature. Pat completely dry with paper towels—this is crucial for a crispy sear. Season both sides generously with sea salt and freshly ground black pepper. Mince 3 cloves of garlic and set aside with lemon juice measured and ready.

2

Sear to Perfection

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Carefully place salmon fillets skin-side up (presentation side down). Press gently with spatula for 10 seconds to ensure even contact. Cook undisturbed for 4 minutes until a golden crust forms. The salmon should release easily from the pan when ready.

3

Create the Magic Sauce

Flip salmon to skin-side down. Immediately add butter to the pan, letting it melt and foam. Add minced garlic and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in fresh lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits. Tilt pan and spoon the bubbling sauce over salmon continuously for 1-2 minutes while salmon finishes cooking.

4

Rest and Serve

Transfer salmon to plates and spoon the remaining lemon-garlic butter sauce over the top. Let rest for 2 minutes to allow juices to redistribute. Serve alongside roasted asparagus and blistered cherry tomatoes. Garnish with fresh parsley and lemon wedges.

👨‍🍳 Chef's Secrets
  • Don't move the salmon during the first 4 minutes—this develops the crust
  • Use a fish spatula for easy flipping without breaking the fillet
  • Baste constantly in the final minute for maximum flavor infusion
  • Internal temp should reach 145°F (63°C) for perfectly cooked salmon
